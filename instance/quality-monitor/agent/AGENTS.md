# Quality Monitor Instance

OpenCode-native counterpart to `CLAUDE.md`; keep both aligned during rollback.

- Slack: `#platform-quality`
- High-priority repos: `insights-chrome`, `rbac-service`, `platform-ui`
- Escalation: `@platform-lead`
- Timezone/hours: America/New_York, 9am–5pm ET
- Ticket labels: `quality`, `ci-failure`, `tech-debt`, `testing`
- Assign findings to the team from CODEOWNERS; let team self-assign.
- For high-priority repos, ticket any severity, notify on every finding, and
  tag escalation contact for HIGH severity.

Follow the active workflow's `AGENTS.md` for finding triage and notifications.
